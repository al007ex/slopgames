Moves

  • Drift: tap Space or Shift as you turn in, then hold W. Steer into the corner for more angle, out of it to straighten.
  • Donut: from a stop, hold a direction and W.
  • Rollback: at speed, hold Space and a direction until you're past sideways, let go of Space and keep the direction and W held. The car whips round, rolls backwards and comes back round.
  • Burnout: from a stop, hold W and S together.
  • Flames: hold Shift and W to bounce the limiter, or F and W for the two-step.

A gamepad works too: triggers for throttle and brake, stick to steer, A handbrake, B clutch, X two-step, bumpers shift.
